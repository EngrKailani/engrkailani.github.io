ROBOTICS / ACADEMIC GROUP ANALYSIS

UR3e robot kinematics

Forward-kinematic and Jacobian analysis for a six-degree-of-freedom industrial manipulator.

Documented finding: The zero-angle configuration produced a rank-five Jacobian, while another analyzed configuration reached full rank. This was mathematical group analysis, not independently validated hardware testing.

INSTRUMENTATION / TEST AUTOMATION

Thermal Controller & Logger

Python-based thermocouple acquisition, live displays, metadata, and structured reporting workflows.

Documented finding: Recovered source supports eight thermocouple channels and CSV/Excel reporting. Published illustrations are synthetic; acquisition accuracy was not revalidated.

CONTROLS / LABORATORY OBSERVATIONS

Control & measurement labs

Comparison of theoretical models and observed laboratory behavior in vibration and control experiments.

Documented finding: A vibration feature was observed near 49.3 Hz. Differences between some control measurements and simulations underscore the need for validation and uncertainty analysis.

EMBEDDED SYSTEMS / GROUP CAPSTONE

IVerify systems integration

A prototype exploring sensing and indication for an IV-tubing monitoring concept.

Documented finding: The group demonstrated a two-LED indication path. A three-state interface remained a goal; clinical accuracy and safety were not validated.
