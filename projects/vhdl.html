VHDL Digital Logic

Binary arithmetic displayed in decimal without a clock

ON THIS PAGEMethods & toolsOutcomesEvidence & limitations
PROJECT 06 / 16

For a digital electronics assignment, I developed purely combinational VHDL circuits that translate binary switch inputs into two-digit seven-segment output. A second design adds two 4-bit numbers and a carry-in and displays the result as a readable decimal value.

Engineering challenge

Convert binary values to decimal display patterns using concurrent hardware logic rather than clocked software-like control.

Methods and approach

Use XOR, AND, and OR equations with full-adder stages and lookup tables for active-low seven-segment displays. The adder produces a five-bit result representing 0 through 31.

Tools and environment

VHDL, combinational logic, full adders, lookup tables, seven-segment displays.
