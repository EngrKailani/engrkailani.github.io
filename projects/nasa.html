PROJECT BRIEF

My role and the result

Problem

Evaluate a robotic-arm concept for chamber access, actuator suitability, and high-vacuum operation.

My contribution

Independently evaluated arm geometry, joint travel, reachable workspace, loading, actuator suitability, preliminary controls, and verification planning under NASA mentor oversight.

Engineering tools

Creo, Python, ROS 2, MuJoCo, actuator load calculations, Pololu Micro Maestro 6, and structured system requirements.

Validation method

Reviewed workspace, actuator loading, thermal behavior, and planned operating scenarios against system requirements.

Documented result

Developed 34 hierarchical requirements from six operating scenarios, calculated an estimated 3.1 N·m shoulder self-load moment for one horizontal configuration, and identified further actuator-loading and vacuum verification needs.

Limitations

The estimated 3.1 N·m shoulder moment excludes rocker and actuator masses, so it is not an actuator-selection result. No project-specific design drawings, calibration data, vacuum qualification or validated operation are presented.

Engineering decisions

Check mechanical feasibility alongside motion control

Estimated a 3.1 N·m shoulder self-load moment for a horizontal configuration; the calculation excludes rocker and actuator masses. The remaining center-of-gravity and joint-loading work must be completed before determining actuator adequacy. Project-specific geometry remains outside this public summary.

Treat thermal behavior as a separate workstream

Motion control alone cannot establish suitability for high vacuum. Steady-state and transient Thermal Desktop analysis addressed the environmental part of the assessment.

Implementation and verification scope

Control & calibration

Developed preliminary control software and atmospheric bench-test procedures for the existing Pololu Micro Maestro 6-channel controller, while documenting further verification needs for replacement vacuum-compatible actuators. ROS 2 and MuJoCo were used in control/simulation development.

Thermal modeling

Used Thermal Desktop for steady-state and transient analysis relevant to high-vacuum operation. The public visual illustrates the workstream qualitatively and is not a solver output.

Requirements & constraints

Developed 34 hierarchical requirements from six operating scenarios addressing manipulation, high-vacuum compatibility, workspace constraints, emergency shutdown and verification. These requirements describe planned engineering work, not completed qualifications.

Verification planning

Connected planned verification activities to system requirements and operating scenarios to support future testing and qualification work.

Assessment status

The assessment identified areas for continued engineering review across actuator capability, mechanical packaging, controls, thermal behavior, and high-vacuum operation. These are design and analytical findings rather than completed acceptance or qualification tests.

Next verification step

Validate actuator assumptions, refine the mechanical and control design, characterize calibration performance, and complete thermal and vacuum verification against the project requirements.
