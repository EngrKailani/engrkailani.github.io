ENGINEERING EVIDENCE / AT A GLANCE

Contribution, evidence, and limitations

Problem

Assess robotic tool handling for a proposed high-vacuum lunar-dust test environment, considering workspace, actuation, thermal constraints, and verification.

My contribution

Independently developed requirements and performed mechanical and systems feasibility analysis, simulation preparation, and control-workflow planning under NASA mentor oversight.

Engineering tools

Requirements mapping, kinematic workspace reasoning, preliminary loading analysis, simulation and controls workflows, and thermal-constraint review.

Validation method

Preliminary requirements-to-verification mapping and analytical checks to identify assumptions requiring future testing. No completed vacuum or robot-hardware validation.

Documented result

A feasibility assessment and verification plan, not an operating or qualified vacuum-ready robotic system.

Limitations

Actuator margins, calibration, thermal suitability, and operation in high vacuum remain unverified. Original NASA drawings and project test data are not public.

My engineering contributions

Systems requirements

Translated operating scenarios into structured requirements addressing manipulation, chamber access, safety, environmental constraints, and planned verification.

Robotics simulation and controls

Developed Python-based simulation and preliminary control tools with visualization and automated task routines. Prepared atmospheric bench-test procedures for legacy control hardware.

Mechanical feasibility

Evaluated arm reach, joint travel, loading assumptions, candidate actuators, and component suitability. The preliminary shoulder-loading estimate is not a final actuator-selection calculation.

Engineering documentation

Prepared technical specifications and verification-planning materials to organize future design reviews and testing work.

Methods and tools

Mechanical and system analysis

Compared task requirements with preliminary workspace and loading analyses to identify constraints needing further design review and testing. No final hardware configuration was qualified.

Environmental and thermal analysis

Explored thermal behavior relevant to high-vacuum mechanisms using Thermal Desktop, alongside conduction-cooling and dust-protection concepts. Public graphics below are conceptual, not solver outputs.

Software and design tools

Applied Creo, Python, ROS-based motion-planning and simulation tools, engineering calculations, and documented procedures. These were development activities, not validated vacuum operation.

Feasibility reasoning / no unreleased project values

From feasibility assumptions to verification needs

A preliminary assessment can identify constraints and the tests needed to resolve them without claiming a qualified design. These are the public, non-quantitative decision areas represented in my internship work.

Workspace and task coverage

Check whether candidate joint travel and reach are compatible with the intended manipulation tasks. A workspace estimate must eventually be compared with verified geometry and test fixtures.

Loading and actuation

Evaluate loading assumptions and their sensitivity to omitted component masses before assessing actuator suitability. A preliminary moment estimate alone cannot establish performance margin.

Thermal and environmental limits

Review heat-transfer pathways, dust exposure, component suitability and controls interfaces. Vacuum-relevant operation requires later component and integrated-system testing.
