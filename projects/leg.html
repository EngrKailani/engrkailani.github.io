Three-Link Human Leg Dynamics

Student-initiated modeling and simulation, mentored by Dr. Sesh Commuri

ENGINEERING EVIDENCE / AT A GLANCE

Contribution, evidence, and limitations

Problem

Model hip, knee and ankle position, velocity and linked-body dynamics.

My contribution

Independently developed a three-link human-leg simulation as a student-initiated Honors Signature Experience with faculty mentorship from Dr. Sesh Commuri.

Engineering tools

MATLAB, Simulink Level-2 S-functions, forward kinematics, Jacobian velocity mapping, and simplified linked-body joint dynamics.

Validation method

Applied analytical kinematic and dynamic relationships within simulation; no independent experimental validation is documented.

Documented result

A three-joint biomechanical simulation exploring position, velocity and joint torque.

Limitations

No prosthetic prototype, measured gait comparison or quantified model error.

FACULTY-MENTORED HONORS RESEARCH · UNR

I modeled the hip, knee, and ankle as a connected three-link human leg. The model translates joint motion into foot position and velocity while examining the torques involved in linked-body motion.

See this Honors Signature Experience in my research overview ↗

Engineering details

Challenge

Calculate the position, velocity, and dynamic response of a three-joint biomechanical system.

Approach

Use a MATLAB S-function within Simulink, Jacobian kinematics, and Newton–Euler dynamics to compute joint torques.

Tools

MATLAB, Simulink, S-functions, Jacobian mapping, and simplified dynamic modeling.

Documented outcome

A faculty-mentored, student-initiated simulation of articulated leg kinematics and simplified dynamics. No assistive device was built, and no physical validation is claimed.

Evidence & limitations

Public source you can inspect: the original MATLAB code file and repository documentation. The Honors research summary records the faculty-mentored scope.

Evidence boundary: Public code supports inspection of the modeling approach, not experimental accuracy. No measured gait comparison, physical prototype, raw experiment dataset, or independent test evidence is represented where unavailable.
