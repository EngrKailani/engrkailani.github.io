3-Link Leg Simulation

Exploring how connected joints move together

EXPLORE THIS PROJECTOverviewApproachFindingsEvidence
PROJECT 03 / 16

I modeled the hip, knee, and ankle as a connected three-link human leg. The model translates joint motion into foot position and velocity while examining the torques involved in linked-body motion.

Engineering details

Challenge

Calculate the position, velocity, and dynamic response of a three-joint biomechanical system.

Approach

Use a MATLAB S-function within Simulink, Jacobian kinematics, and Newton–Euler dynamics to compute joint torques.

Tools

MATLAB, Simulink, S-functions, Jacobians, Newton–Euler dynamics.

Documented outcome

An Honors Contract modeling project that strengthened my interest in future prosthetics and exoskeleton research; no assistive device was built.
