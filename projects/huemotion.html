HueMotion

Turning hand gestures into lighting responses

ON THIS PAGEMethods & toolsOutcomesEvidence & limitations
PROJECT 14 / 16

HueMotion explores what happens when a familiar action becomes an interface. By interpreting hand gestures from a webcam, the project connects human movement to responsive RGBW lighting.

Engineering challenge

Make gesture recognition useful for controlling lighting in an intuitive way.

Methods and approach

Track hands with MediaPipe and map gesture states to lighting controls and a browser preview.

Tools and environment

JavaScript, MediaPipe, Node.js, webcam tracking, RGBW lighting.
