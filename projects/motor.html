PROJECT 05 / 16

Working with an STM32F767ZI introduced the practical side of motor timing: configuring timer outputs, changing pulse-width modulation, and gathering response data to understand hardware behavior. Related microprocessor lab work also covered push-button inputs and direct GPIO control.

Engineering details

Challenge

Generate controlled timing signals and relate PWM behavior to physical motor responses.

Approach

Develop STM32 firmware for PWM-based motor control and encoder capture; examine logged motor-response timing. A related lab example sweeps the TIM3 channel 2 compare register to vary duty cycle.

Tools

STM32F767ZI, embedded C, hardware timers, PWM, encoder capture, GPIO.

Documented outcome

Experience connecting microcontroller peripherals, control timing, and experimental observations.
