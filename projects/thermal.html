Thermal Controller & Logger

Making temperature measurements easier to trust

ON THIS PAGEMethods & toolsOutcomesEvidence & limitations
PROJECT 10 / 16

I built tools to take repetitive engineering thermal tests and make them easier to monitor, compare, and document. The interesting part was not just collecting numbers, but helping engineers recognize stable conditions and produce repeatable reports.

Engineering challenge

Manual monitoring and inconsistent reporting slow down thermal validation.

Methods and approach

Combine Python data collection and live visualization with Arduino ambient control, stability tracking, and structured exports.

Tools and environment

Python, Arduino, Tkinter, temperature instrumentation, CSV and Excel.
