Pipelined VHDL ALU

A clocked four-bit arithmetic and logic unit

ON THIS PAGEMethods & toolsOutcomesEvidence & limitations
PROJECT 07 / 16

I designed and tested a four-bit arithmetic and logic unit from a high-level datapath down to the individual logic blocks. Four chained one-bit full adders form the arithmetic core; registered inputs and outputs make the results stable and predictable. The design uses board switches and a pushbutton for commands, with LEDs and seven-segment displays as visible feedback.

Engineering challenge

Implement multiple arithmetic and logic operations with a one-clock-cycle registered interface and clearly observable hardware outputs.

Methods and approach

Combine a four-bit ripple-carry adder, operand selection logic, an XOR path, input/output flip-flops and a seven-segment decoder. Test pass-through, increment, addition, subtraction and XOR operations with representative switch settings.

Tools and environment

VHDL, ripple-carry adder, sequential logic, FPGA I/O mapping, binary LED readout, seven-segment display.

Evidence and limitations

Documented outcome

A tested synchronous design with outputs updating on the following clock cycle. Debugging pin assignments and competing LED drivers reinforced disciplined hardware integration.
