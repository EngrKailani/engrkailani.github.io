UR3e Robot Kinematics

Modeling six degrees of freedom with Jacobians

ON THIS PAGEMethods & toolsOutcomesEvidence & limitations
PROJECT 02 / 16

In a three-person robotics analysis project, our team studied the Universal Robots UR3e, a six-joint collaborative arm. We assigned Denavit–Hartenberg frames and derived the transformations that describe how each joint contributes to the end-effector pose. The project connected the mathematics of robot motion to an industrial manipulator.

My contribution / team context

Member of a three-person robot-kinematics analysis team; the project documentation describes shared DH, Jacobian, and singularity analysis.

Engineering challenge

Represent the pose and end-effector velocity of a six-revolute-joint robot and identify singular configurations.

Methods and approach

Develop a six-link DH table, multiply homogeneous transformations, derive the Jacobian, and evaluate its rank with MATLAB. The analyzed zero-angle configuration produced rank 5, indicating a singularity.

Tools and environment

MATLAB, Denavit–Hartenberg convention, forward kinematics, Jacobians, velocity analysis.

Documented outcome

A group analysis of end-effector motion and singularity, with another evaluated configuration reaching full Jacobian rank.
