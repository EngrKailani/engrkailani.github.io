NASA Marshall · Robotic systems · 2026

Robotic-arm feasibility for lunar-dust testing

Problem: Assess the feasibility of robotic tool handling in a proposed high-vacuum lunar-dust test chamber. My role: Individual systems analysis, requirements, and simulation under NASA mentor oversight.

Methods and results Workspace and joint-loading analysis; Python, ROS 2, and MoveIt 2 simulation; 34 hierarchical requirements and a preliminary 3.1 N·m shoulder self-load estimate. Not a vacuum qualification.

Research direction

Reliable manipulation in uncertain environments.

I want to understand when robots can safely complete manipulation tasks despite incomplete sensing and variable contact. My interests include sensor fusion, state estimation, and contact-aware control for lunar and planetary operations, including work alongside astronauts.

These are research interests and proposed questions, not claims of completed doctoral experiments. My publicly documented project work is separated from future research concepts.
