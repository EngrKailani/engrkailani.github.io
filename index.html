Research direction

Reliable manipulation in uncertain environments.

My research interests center on how space robots can manipulate tools reliably despite uncertain sensing and changing contact. I hope to investigate state estimation, sensor fusion, and contact-aware control for lunar and planetary operations alongside astronauts.

These are proposed doctoral research directions; my documented engineering work appears in the project portfolio.
