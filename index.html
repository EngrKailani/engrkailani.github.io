Research direction

Reliable manipulation in uncertain environments.

I want to understand when robots can safely complete manipulation tasks despite incomplete sensing and variable contact. My interests include sensor fusion, state estimation, and contact-aware control for lunar and planetary operations, including work alongside astronauts.

These are research interests and proposed questions, not claims of completed doctoral experiments. My publicly documented project work is separated from future research concepts.
